Looking for a hotel with breakfast available on the Costa Brava shouldn't be complicated, but the reality is that many accommodations offer a limited menu breakfast or sell it as an extra at disproportionate prices. In Tossa de Mar, however, there is a different hospitality tradition: breakfast as a moment of enjoyment, not as a separate business.

At our accommodations, breakfast is **available as an optional service** and **is not included in the room rate**: you can add it to your stay if you wish, with local produce and at an adjusted price.

## Breakfast on the Costa Brava: more than a buffet

In Catalan gastronomic culture, breakfast has a special importance. It is not just about having something quick before going out: it is the moment to plan the day, enjoy local products and connect with the surroundings. A good breakfast on the Costa Brava should include:

- **Pa amb tomàquet:** toasted bread with tomato and olive oil, the quintessential Catalan breakfast.
- **Local cold meats:** fuet from Vic, serrano ham and goat's cheese from the area.
- **Sea products:** in some accommodations, breakfast includes anchovies from l'Escala or esqueixada cod.
- **Fresh seasonal fruit:** peaches, figs and oranges from the Maresme.
- **Artisan roasted coffee:** the Costa Brava has several renowned local roasters.

## Why Tossa de Mar stands out for accommodation with breakfast

Tossa de Mar is not a mass destination like Lloret de Mar or Blanes. This is noticeable in the quality of service: family-run accommodations have time and motivation to look after the details. Breakfast is not just another line on the bill: it is a way of demonstrating hospitality.

### Advantages of adding breakfast to your stay in Tossa

- **Good value for money:** for an adjusted price, you have breakfast with local produce without leaving your accommodation.
- **Convenience:** you don't need to go out looking for breakfast. Start the day relaxed, in no hurry.
- **Local produce:** the best hostels in the centre work with local bakeries and suppliers.
- **Flexible times:** unlike large hotels, family hostels usually offer wider breakfast hours.

## Where to find the best breakfast in Tossa de Mar

Although there are excellent cafés in the centre, such as Pasteleria Serra or Bar la Vila, staying in a hostel with breakfast available allows you to combine comfort and quality. At Hostalet de Tossa, for example, breakfast is served in a bright space in the main building, with views over the cobbled streets of the old town.

Our breakfast includes:
- Freshly baked bread from Triticum bakery in Tossa.
- Selection of cold meats and cheeses from the Garrotxa region.
- Fresh fruit, natural yoghurt and cereals.
- Freshly ground coffee, tea and hot chocolate.
- Vegetarian and gluten-free options available on request.

## Tips for booking your hotel with breakfast on the Costa Brava

1. **Read the reviews:** guests often specifically value the quality of breakfast. Look for mentions of fresh produce and variety.
2. **Ask about special options:** if you have food allergies or vegetarian preferences, confirm availability before booking.
3. **Avoid intermediaries:** booking directly on the accommodation's website lets you add breakfast easily and contact the hotel with no middlemen.
4. **Check the times:** in summer, some accommodations extend breakfast hours until 11:00 so you can sleep in.

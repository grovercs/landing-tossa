Tossa de Mar, often known as the "Pearl of the Costa Brava", is undoubtedly one of the most magical destinations in Catalonia. Its walled enclosure, the only preserved fortified medieval town on the Catalan coast, enchants all its visitors. If you wonder **what to see in Tossa de Mar** and how to organise your stay, this complete guide is your ideal starting point for an unforgettable holiday.

From Hostalet de Tossa and El Bergantí, located in the heart of town, we want to share our local perspective.

## 1. The Vila Vella and Tossa Castle

The walled enclosure of the <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Vila+Vella+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vila Vella</a> is the undisputed emblem of Tossa de Mar. Walking through its narrow, steep cobbled streets transports you directly to medieval times. The imposing walls, built between the 12th and 14th centuries to protect the local population, offer today impressive panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Inside the enclosure you will find the church of Sant Vicenç, the Municipal Museum with the famous work "La Venus de Tossa" by Lluís Rigalt, and multiple viewpoints from which to contemplate the bay.

*Local tip:* we highly recommend climbing up at sunset to enjoy one of the best sunsets on the Costa Brava. For historical context, visit the <a href="https://www.infotossa.com/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Official Tossa de Mar Tourism Office</a>.

## 2. The Lighthouse and Roman Villa

Crowning the old town is the <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Faro+de+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tossa Lighthouse</a>. From here you get the best panoramic views. If you love ancient history, don't miss the <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Villa+Romana+dels+Ametllers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Roman Villa dels Ametllers</a>, a fascinating archaeological site right in the town centre dating from the 1st century AD.

## 3. Best beaches and coves

Tossa de Mar has a variety of beaches for all tastes:

- **Gran Beach:** the main one, with complete services and coarse sand ideal for families.
- **Mar Menuda Beach:** more intimate, perfect for snorkelling.
- **Cala Pola:** accessible via the Camí de Ronda, a virgin cove with crystal-clear waters.
- **Cala Giverola:** with services and access by glass-bottom boat.

Check our <a href="/en/blogs/playas-calas-tossa">complete guide to beaches and coves</a> for more details.

## 4. Must-do activities

### The Tourist Train (Carrilet)
Tours the town and climbs up to the Lighthouse. Ideal for families with children and those who prefer to avoid the uphill walk.

### Diving and snorkelling
The transparent waters of the Costa Brava are perfect for observing marine life. Mar Menuda Beach is the top diving spot.

### Hiking the Camí de Ronda
Spectacular routes connecting Tossa with virgin coves. The northern route to Cala Pola is the most popular. Discover more in our <a href="/en/blogs/senderismo-tossa">hiking guide</a>.

### Local gastronomy
You cannot leave without trying Cim i Tomba, the traditional fishermen's dish. And of course, tapas in the centre. Read our <a href="/en/blogs/que-comer-tossa">gastronomic guide</a>.

## Practical tips for your visit

1. **Best season:** May, June, September and October offer ideal weather and fewer crowds.
2. **Parking:** difficult in August. We recommend staying in the centre and forgetting the car.
3. **Footwear:** cobbled streets require comfortable shoes.
4. **Book in advance:** centre accommodations fill up quickly in high season.

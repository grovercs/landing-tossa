Tossa de Mar is undoubtedly one of the most romantic destinations on the Costa Brava. Its cobbled streets, Mediterranean views and tranquil atmosphere make it the perfect setting for a couples' getaway. From Hostalet de Tossa and El Bergantí, located in the heart of the town, we propose a complete plan for an unforgettable romantic escape.

## Why Tossa de Mar is perfect for couples

Unlike other Costa Brava destinations, Tossa de Mar has maintained its essence as a fishing village. There are no large impersonal hotels or overwhelming crowds. Instead you will find intimate alleyways, hidden corners and a golden light at sunset that transforms every corner into a postcard.

### Intimate and exclusive atmosphere

- **The Vila Vella at dusk:** when the day-trippers leave, the old town recovers a magical calm. Walking along its walls with sea views is an experience that is priceless.
- **Quiet beaches:** unlike Lloret or Blanes, Tossa's beaches are not saturated. You will find corners where you can enjoy the sun and sea in privacy.
- **Gastronomy to share:** the port and centre restaurants offer intimate tables with sea views, perfect for a candlelit dinner.

## 48-hour romantic getaway plan

### Day 1: Arrival and sunset at the Vila Vella

- **16:00 — Check-in:** arrive at your accommodation in the centre. At Hostalet de Tossa we will welcome you with a bottle of chilled cava and a selection of artisan chocolates from the Costa Brava.
- **18:00 — Stroll through the old town:** walk along the walls of the Vila Vella. From the lighthouse viewpoint the sea views are spectacular.
- **20:00 — Romantic dinner:** book a table at one of the harbour restaurants with a terrace over the sea. The fresh seafood menu is a must.
- **22:00 — Drink under the stars:** end the night at one of the intimate terraces in the centre with a gin and tonic or rosé cava.

### Day 2: Breakfast with views and an exclusive beach

- **09:00 — Breakfast at the accommodation:** our breakfast includes local produce served in a quiet space. Start the day at your leisure.
- **11:00 — Gran Beach or Cala Pola:** if you prefer atmosphere, Gran Beach is a 2-minute walk away. If you seek exclusivity, Cala Pola, a 30-minute walk along the Camí de Ronda, is a virgin paradise.
- **14:00 — Tapas by the sea:** Bar La Vila or the terrace at Club Nàutic are perfect for an informal lunch with views.
- **17:00 — Spa or couples massage:** although Tossa does not have large spas, some nearby hotels offer treatments for non-guests. Ask us and we will manage the booking.
- **20:30 — Farewell dinner:** close your getaway at a Vila Vella restaurant with night-time sea views.

## Where to stay for a romantic getaway

For a couples' escape, location is key. You will want to be close to everything so you can stroll without needing a car, but also in a tranquil environment.

- **Hostalet de Tossa:** newly renovated double rooms with private bathroom, fans and welcome details for couples. A 2-minute walk from the beach and a step away from the best restaurants.
- **El Bergantí:** a more bohemian and youthful atmosphere, ideal for young couples seeking close treatment and competitive prices without sacrificing the charm of the centre.

Both properties offer direct booking with the **best guaranteed price**, without intermediaries or commissions.

## Tips for a perfect romantic getaway

1. **Book in advance:** spring and autumn weekends are the most in-demand by couples. With at least two weeks' notice you will ensure availability and a better price.
2. **Request the welcome detail:** when booking directly, let us know it is a romantic getaway and we will prepare the room with a special touch.
3. **Avoid August:** if you seek intimacy, July and August are the most crowded months. May, June, September and October offer ideal weather and much more tranquillity.
4. **Check the events calendar:** Tossa celebrates music festivals and medieval markets that can enrich your getaway.

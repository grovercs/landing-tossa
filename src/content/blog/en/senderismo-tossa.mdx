For nature and trekking lovers, **hiking in Tossa de Mar** is an unforgettable experience. The Camí de Ronda, an ancient route historically used by coastguards to watch for smuggling, now borders the coastline offering aerial views of cliffs, virgin coves and the Mediterranean in all its splendour.

## Northern Route: Tossa to Cala Pola (medium level)

The most popular Camí de Ronda hike starts from <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja+de+la+Mar+Menuda" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mar Menuda Beach</a>. The trail climbs quickly offering unbeatable views of Tossa bay until you reach the beautiful <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala+Pola+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cala Pola</a>, where you can take a well-deserved dip in crystal-clear waters.

**Technical data:**
- **Distance:** 4 km (one way)
- **Duration:** 1h 30min (one way)
- **Elevation:** +180 metres
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Best season:** All year. In summer, leave before 9:00 to avoid heat.

**Safety:** download the official GPS track from <a href="https://es.wikiloc.com/rutas/senderismo/espana/catalunya/tossa-de-mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wikiloc (Routes in Tossa)</a>.

## Southern Route: Tossa to Lloret de Mar (high level)

For more experienced hikers, the southern route is longer and more demanding. It crosses a dense mountain massif, passing the impressive Mirador des Cars, then descends to solitary beaches like <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala+Morisca+Tossa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cala Morisca</a>.

**Technical data:**
- **Distance:** 12 km (one way)
- **Duration:** 4 hours (one way)
- **Elevation:** +450 metres
- **Difficulty:** High

## Short routes for all abilities

- **Vila Vella to the Lighthouse:** 20 minutes gentle climb with panoramic views. Ideal for families.
- **Promenade to Cala Futadera:** 30 minutes flat walk with cliff views.
- **Viewpoint Route:** 1 hour circular linking three viewpoints over the bay.

## Essential hiking equipment for Tossa

| Equipment | Recommendation | Why |
|-----------|----------------|-----|
| Footwear | Trekking shoes with grip | Some sections are rocky and slippery |
| Water | 1.5 litres minimum | No fountains on the route |
| Protection | Cap, sunglasses, SPF 50+ | Mediterranean sun is intense |
| Snorkel | Mask and tube | Cala Pola and Cala Morisca are perfect |
| GPS | Wikiloc app downloaded | Mobile coverage limited on cliff sections |

## Where to stay if you come hiking

For hikers, the ideal accommodation needs three things: central location (to start the route without car journeys), hot shower on return and substantial breakfast before setting off.

- **Hostalet de Tossa:** rooms with private bathroom, good pressure shower and included breakfast with energy-rich foods. A 2-minute walk from the northern Camí de Ronda starting point.
- **El Bergantí:** economical option for groups of hiker friends.

Both offer **direct booking with best guaranteed price**.

## Tips for hikers

1. Avoid midday hours in summer.
2. Don't rely solely on Google Maps: use Wikiloc.
3. Bring food: Cala Pola has a kiosk in season, Cala Morisca has nothing.

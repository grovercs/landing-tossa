Tossa's gastronomy is deeply linked to the sea and the maritime traditions of the Costa Brava. 

## The Star Dish: Cim i Tomba
You cannot visit without trying <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cim_i_tomba" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cim i Tomba</a>, the town's traditional dish par excellence. Century-old places like <a href="https://www.restaurantminerva.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Restaurant Minerva</a> are famous for this dish.

## Tapas, Seafood, and Rice Dishes
Seafood rice dishes and fresh grilled seafood never fail. For snacking, L'Escala anchovy tapas and Roman-style squid are mandatory.

## Terrace and Breakfast at Hostalet de Tossa
If you are looking for a more intimate and evocative experience, <a href="https://www.hostalhostalet.com/es/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hostalet de Tossa</a> invites you to enjoy its charming terrace surrounded by greenery, next to the iconic Sant Vicenç church. It is the perfect place to start the day with a quiet breakfast or relax with a book in the afternoon, immersed in the history of an 18th-century house in the heart of the Vila Vella.

## El Bergantí Tapas Restaurant
And if you want to taste authentic local gastronomy without leaving the center, <a href="https://elberganti-tossa.com/en/restaurant/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">El Bergantí Restaurant</a> prepares traditional specialties and tapas daily.

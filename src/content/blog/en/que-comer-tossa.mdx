Tossa de Mar's gastronomy is deeply linked to the sea and the maritime traditions of the Costa Brava. If you're wondering **what to eat in Tossa de Mar** during your stay, this guide reveals the must-try dishes, the most charming restaurants and the culinary secrets that only locals know.

## The star dish: Cim i Tomba

You cannot visit Tossa de Mar without trying the historic <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cim_i_tomba" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cim i Tomba</a>. Its origins date back to ancient local fishermen, who prepared this survival stew on their own boats. Made with rock fish, potatoes, onion, garlic, tomato and finished with a smooth aioli, century-old establishments like <a href="https://www.restaurantminerva.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Restaurant Minerva</a> are famous for their dedication to this traditional fisherman's stew.

## Tapas, seafood and rice dishes: the best of marine cuisine

- **Seafood rice dishes and fideuás:** made with fresh fish from Palamós fish market.
- **Fresh seafood grills:** prawns, crayfish and clams grilled, served on terraces overlooking the harbour.
- **L'Escala anchovy tapas:** considered the best in the Mediterranean, served on toasted bread with olive oil.
- **Roman-style squid:** crispy on the outside and tender on the inside.
- **Pa amb tomàquet:** the quintessential Catalan breakfast and appetiser.

## Where to breakfast in Tossa de Mar

- **Pastelería Serra:** freshly baked croissants and artisan roasted coffee in the pedestrian centre.
- **Bar La Vila:** traditional Catalan breakfast with pa amb tomàquet and local cold meats.

To make your holidays in Tossa de Mar as comfortable as possible, we have prepared this **practical information guide** with everything you need to know before arriving: buses from Barcelona and Girona, where to park, on-duty pharmacies, supermarkets and medical care.

## How to get to Tossa de Mar

### By bus (Moventis)
Tossa de Mar is excellently connected by bus with the main Catalan cities:
- **From Barcelona:** direct line from Estación del Nord. Duration: approximately 1h 30min.
- **From Girona:** frequent connections. Duration: 45 min.
- **From Lloret de Mar:** local lines every 30 minutes in high season.

Check updated schedules on the <a href="https://www.moventis.es/en/search/node/tossa%20de%20mar%20language%3Aen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official Moventis search page for Tossa de Mar</a>.

### By car
- **From Barcelona:** AP-7 to exit 9 (Lloret), then GI-600 to Tossa. About 85 km.
- **From Girona:** N-II and GI-681. About 45 km.

## Parking in Tossa de Mar

| Parking area | Location | Approx. price | Recommendation |
|--------------|----------|---------------|----------------|
| Municipal Parking | Town entrance, blue zone | €1-2/h | Ideal for day visits |
| Esplanada Parking | Next to bus station | €15-20/day | More economical for long stays |
| Blue zone centre | Urban streets | €1.50/h | Very limited, difficult in August |
| Free parking | Periphery, 10-15 min walk | Free | Recommended if no hotel parking |

**Local tip:** if you stay in the historic centre (like at Hostalet de Tossa or El Bergantí), forget the car.

## Supermarkets and food shopping

- **<a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Esclat+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Esclat / Bon Preu</a>:** the largest supermarket at the town entrance. Open 8:30-21:00.
- **<a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Caprabo+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Caprabo</a>:** very central, near the bus station. Open 9:00-21:30.
- **Weekly market:** Tuesday mornings on Avenida República Argentina.

## Pharmacies and medical care

### Pharmacies in Tossa de Mar
- **Farmàcia Doria:** the historic town pharmacy on the main street. Open 9:00-21:00.
- **Farmàcia Costa Brava:** on Avenida República Argentina.

### On-duty pharmacy service
Check the <a href="https://www.gencat.cat/web/.content/home/03_el_govern/07_conselleries/06_salut/farmaciesguardia/farmaciesguardia.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official Catalonia on-duty pharmacy calendar</a>.

### Medical care
- **<a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/CAP+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CAP Tossa de Mar</a>:** primary care centre for non-serious emergencies. Phone: 972 340 202.
- **Palamós Hospital:** nearest reference hospital, 25 km away.
- **Emergencies:** call 112.

## Additional useful services

- **Tourist office:** at the Vila Vella entrance. Open 9:00-21:00 in summer.
- **Bike rental:** several shops in the centre. Price: approx. €15/day.
- **Laundry:** on Calle de la Guardia Civil.
- **ATMs:** CaixaBank and Santander on the main avenue.

## Why staying in the centre is the best logistical decision

After reading all this practical information, one conclusion is clear: **staying in Tossa de Mar's centre simplifies everything**. No car needed, pharmacies, supermarkets and restaurants at your doorstep, and you can return to your accommodation at any time.

From **Hostalet de Tossa** and **El Bergantí**, located in the heart of town, you have:
- Bus station 5 minutes on foot.
- Supermarkets 3 minutes away.
- Pharmacies 2 minutes away.
- Gran Beach and Vila Vella 2 minutes away.

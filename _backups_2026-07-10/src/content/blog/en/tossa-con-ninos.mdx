Spending **holidays in Tossa de Mar with kids** is one of the best decisions you can make for a family trip on the Costa Brava. Unlike neighbouring destinations more focused on nightlife, Tossa maintains a safe, relaxed atmosphere full of activities designed for the little ones to enjoy as much as the adults.

## Safe and comfortable beaches for kids

### Gran Beach
<a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja+Gran+Tossa+de+Mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gran Beach</a> is the ideal option for families. It has clean and shallow water at the shore, complete lifeguard services throughout the summer, sun lounger rental, showers and toilets. Access is flat with ramps for pushchairs.

A great advantage of Tossa is its sand type: unlike the fine sand of other beaches, the coarse sand at Gran Beach **doesn't stick excessively to the skin or fly in the wind**, making beach logistics and cleanup much easier.

### Mar Menuda Beach
Smaller and protected, <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja+de+la+Mar+Menuda" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mar Menuda Beach</a> is perfect for small children thanks to its enclosed corner where the water is extremely calm, like a natural pool.

## Essential kids' activities and adventures

### The Tourist Train (Carrilet)
An absolute must-do activity. Children love riding the tourist train that tours the town and climbs up to Tossa Lighthouse, saving them the tough uphill walk. The journey lasts about 20 minutes and offers panoramic views that will amaze both young and old.

### Glass-Bottom Boats
Departing constantly from Gran Beach, these boats are a fantastic way for children to observe marine life — fish, starfish and posidonia meadows — without getting wet. Discover more at <a href="https://www.fondocristal.com/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fondo de Cristal Tossa</a>.

### Vila Vella: a medieval castle to explore
The walled old town is a fairy-tale setting for children. Narrow alleyways, watchtowers, ancient cannons and sea views from the walls. Bring a torch if you visit at dusk: the lighting on the stone streets creates a magical atmosphere.

### Children's Playground on Paseo del Mar
In the shade of the pines next to Gran Beach, there is a playground with swings, slides and play areas for different ages. Perfect for children to socialise while adults rest on nearby benches.

## Where to stay in Tossa de Mar with kids

Choosing the right accommodation makes all the difference on a family trip. Here are the advantages of staying in the centre:

- **Walking access to the beach:** forget carrying chairs, umbrellas and toys for kilometres. From the centre you reach Gran Beach in 2 minutes.
- **Possibility to return to your accommodation:** for midday naps, changing the kids or preparing a quick sandwich.
- **Safe evening strolls:** the pedestrian centre is safe for children to run and play.
- **Breakfast included:** at Hostalet de Tossa we serve breakfast adapted for families.

**Hostalet de Tossa** is the ideal option for families: spacious rooms, private bathroom, air conditioning and breakfast included. We are at street level in the very centre, with no stairs or steep slopes from the beach.

## Practical tips for families

1. **Best season:** June and September are ideal. Water is warm, fewer crowds and prices are more affordable.
2. **Sun protection:** Mediterranean sun is intense. Use SPF 50+ and UV protection umbrellas.
3. **Footwear:** centre cobblestones and cove rocks require closed sandals or trainers.
4. **Book in advance:** family accommodation in the centre fills up first.

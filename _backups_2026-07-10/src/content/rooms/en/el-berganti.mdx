### Accommodation and Restaurant in the Heart of Tossa

**El Bergantí** offers you an unbeatable location in the center of Tossa de Mar, ideal for those looking to explore the Vila Vella and have the beach just a few minutes' walk away.

It stands out for its **own restaurant with a maritime atmosphere and nautical decor**, specializing in tapas, rice dishes, and Mediterranean cuisine made with local products. It is the perfect option to combine a comfortable stay with an authentic gastronomic experience on the Costa Brava.

### Comfortable Rooms

Our rooms are designed to guarantee your rest. They feature a private bathroom, air conditioning, TV, and free Wi-Fi connection. Their simple and functional design makes them the ideal refuge after a day exploring the cobbled streets of Tossa or sunbathing on the beach.

- **Location:** Central, close to the beach and shopping area.
- **Gastronomy:** Tapas, rice dishes, and Mediterranean plates in the ground floor restaurant.
- **Service:** Family-friendly, direct, and professional.

Choose El Bergantí if you are looking for comfort, good food, and the purest essence of Tossa de Mar without leaving your accommodation.

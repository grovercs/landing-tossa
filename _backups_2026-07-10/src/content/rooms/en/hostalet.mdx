### A Charming Hostel with Great History

The **Hostalet de Tossa** is located in one of the oldest and most beautiful houses in Tossa de Mar, built in 1789. This historic jewel is located in a super quiet inner square of the old town, isolated from the hustle and bustle but just a few minutes from the beach.

What makes the Hostalet special is its intimate and evocative atmosphere. Its beautiful **terrace surrounded by greenery** is located next to the iconic parish church of Sant Vicenç, making it the perfect place to enjoy a good breakfast in the morning or relax reading a book in the afternoon.

### Simplicity and Warmth

The rooms of the Hostalet have been designed keeping the original charm of the building, combining it with the modern comforts necessary for a perfect stay: full private bathroom, air conditioning, television, and Wi-Fi.

- **Location:** Old town (Vila Vella), next to the church, in a noise-free area.
- **Environment:** Exclusive terrace with a bohemian and romantic atmosphere.
- **Service:** Close, careful, and attentive to every detail.

If you are looking to disconnect in a corner with soul and history, a step away from the castle and the sea, the Hostalet de Tossa is your ideal refuge.
